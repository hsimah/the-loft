# Names (D1)

Proposals following the [naming rules](../../CLAUDE.md#names). Containers inside a bundle keep product names (`silverbullet`, `radicale`, `mealie`, `paperless`); helpers take the group prefix (`<vault>-db`, `<vault>-fetch`).

**No name needed**: ntfy (Houstn `notify` profile), the media worker (Sputnik profile, `sputnik-worker`), the reports (n8n workflows in Sputnik).

## Open brain bundle: wiki, calendar, recipes as profiles

- `huddl` — the pack huddles round shared notes.
- `packr` — everything the pack keeps together.
- `mir` — the space station the crew lived in; Russian for both "world" and "peace".

## Vault

- `goldn` — Voyager's Golden Record: a sealed archive built to last.
