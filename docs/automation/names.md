# Names (D1)

Decided 2026-10-25. Follows the [naming rules](../../CLAUDE.md#names): a primary application keeps the service name and helpers take it as a prefix; a bundle of products uses product names. Each is a Roman god that is also, or sounds like, a body in the solar system.

| Service | Name | Containers |
|---|---|---|
| Wiki and documents ([Mercurius](mercurius.md)) | `mercurius`: god of commerce, eloquence and communication; the planet Mercury | Product names: `silverbullet`, `paperless`, `paperless-gpt`; helpers take the bundle prefix: `mercurius-db`, `mercurius-redis`, `mercurius-tika`, `mercurius-gotenberg`, `mercurius-fetch` |
| Calendar ([Janus](janus.md)) | `janus`: god of beginnings and doorways, who gives January its name; a moon of Saturn | `janus` (Radicale), `janus-git` |
| Recipes ([Ceres](ceres.md)) | `ceres`: goddess of grain and harvest; a dwarf planet | `ceres` (Mealie), helpers for the reel pipeline as needed |

**No name needed**: ntfy (Houstn `notify` profile), the media worker (Sputnik profile, `sputnik-worker`), the reports (n8n workflows in Sputnik).

Intake addresses, if Cloudflare routing is used ([intake](intake.md)): open.

Public guest wiki ([Mercurius](mercurius.md#later)): open.
